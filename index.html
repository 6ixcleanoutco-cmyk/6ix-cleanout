How it works

From photo to cleared space in four steps.

STEP 1

Describe the job

Pick how much truck space it needs or list the items. The ticket updates as you go.

Your answers become a job assessment: crew size, time on site, truck loads and anything that needs special handling.

The crew carries everything out, sorts it for donation and recycling where possible, and leaves the space swept.
